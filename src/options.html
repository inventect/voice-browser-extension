Settings

Voice Browser turns what you say into browser actions. It asks TypeSafe’s Jev model a handful of yes/no and multiple-choice questions about your words and the page — in about a third of a second — and then acts in your current tab.

TypeSafe API key

Get a key at console.typesafe.ai/keys. It is stored only on this device and sent only to api.typesafe.ai.

Speech recognition · 음성 인식

Chrome’s built-in recogniser is free but weak on short Korean commands. With an ElevenLabs API key the extension uses Scribe v2 Realtime and tells it the words on the page you are controlling. The key is stored only on this device; the side panel only ever gets 15-minute single-use tokens. Audio then goes to ElevenLabs instead of Google, billed per second while the mic is on (about $0.39 per hour; the mic pauses after 3 quiet minutes).

How it works

1You speak

ElevenLabs Scribe (when its key is saved) or Chrome’s speech service turns your voice into text, word by word.

2Jev decides

Your words plus a compact list of the page’s buttons and links are sent to Jev, which answers typed questions — no text generation.

3Chrome acts

Code applies fixed thresholds and clicks, types, scrolls or navigates in your active tab.

Privacy

Your API keys never leave this device except as a header to their own service (TypeSafe; ElevenLabs, which returns short-lived single-use tokens for the side panel). Audio goes to ElevenLabs when its key is saved, otherwise to Chrome’s Web Speech service (Google); with ElevenLabs, up to 50 short visible labels of the page you are controlling are sent along as spelling hints. The command transcript, active page URL and title, and a compact list of actionable element labels go to TypeSafe. Password fields and user-entered form values are excluded. Provider retention is governed by their terms. No analytics, no remote code. Actions that look irreversible (buy, delete, send, post) wait for you to say “confirm”.
